About SureHands

An agency built
on trust, not pressure.

SureHands Agency exists for one reason: to help Singapore families plan home care support the right way — through honest guidance, careful matching and long-term support.

We started SureHands because we kept hearing the same story from friends and neighbours — rushed decisions, unclear information, and agencies that disappeared the moment the contract was signed. We believed it could be done differently.

Today, we serve hundreds of Singaporean households the way we'd want to be served ourselves: patiently, transparently, and with deep respect for both the family and the helper.

Our Process

Five careful steps,
one peaceful outcome.

No rushing, no pressure. Just a calm, structured process from first conversation to settled routine.

  1. 01

    Understand your household needs

    A relaxed conversation — usually on WhatsApp or in person — to understand who lives at home, daily routines, special care needs, and the kind of person who would fit naturally with your family.

  2. 02

    Share suitable candidates privately

    We hand-pick a small shortlist — never an overwhelming list of options. Each candidate comes with a full, honest profile: experience, language ability, family situation, and our personal notes from interviewing them. Candidate details are shared only with your family, on request.

  3. 03

    Interview & selection

    Video or in-person interviews, with our consultant present to translate, clarify, and guide. Take your time — we'd rather you meet a few candidates than rush into a decision you'll second-guess.

  4. 04

    Documentation & onboarding

    We handle MOM applications, medical exams, insurance, and airport pickup. Before your helper arrives, we'll brief you on first-week expectations, house rules, and how to set a calm tone from day one.

  5. 05

    Aftercare & support

    We stay involved long after placement — checking in regularly, mediating misunderstandings, and supporting both family and helper. Your service agreement sets out the support that applies — we walk you through it before you sign.

How do I get full details for my situation? +

Book a consultation. We'll explain the process, timelines and our service agreement in writing, and answer any questions before you decide.

What happens if things don't work out? +

We stay involved after placement with regular check-ins, and we always mediate first — most misunderstandings resolve with a calm conversation. Your service agreement sets out the support that applies, and we walk you through it before you sign.

How long does the whole process take? +

A transfer helper can typically arrive in 1–3 weeks. New helpers from overseas usually take 4–8 weeks once paperwork begins. We'll give you realistic timelines from day one.

I'm a first-time employer — is this complicated? +

Not at all. We'll guide you through MOM's Employer Orientation Programme, contracts, house rules, and what to expect in your helper's first week. No question is too basic.

What documents will I need? +

For employers: NRIC, proof of address, and income documentation. For renewals: existing work permit and helper's passport. We handle everything else — work permit application, medical exams, insurance, and onboarding paperwork — on your behalf.
